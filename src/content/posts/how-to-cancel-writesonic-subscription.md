## The short answer

Go to **app.writesonic.com/settings/billing**, open **My Subscription**, click **Cancel Subscription**, and confirm. That is the whole process — about two minutes. You keep access to your paid plan until the end of the current billing cycle, and you can claim a refund within 7 days of the charge under Writesonic's Terms (section 12.6). If you subscribed through Apple or Google Play, you must cancel in the App Store or Google Play instead — the Writesonic dashboard cannot cancel an app-store subscription.

## Cancel, step by step

**1. Log in at app.writesonic.com.** Use the account that holds the subscription. If you have more than one Writesonic account — a common mistake when people sign up with Google and then with email — make sure you are in the one that is actually being charged.

**2. Open Settings, then Billing.** The path is **Settings → Billing**, or go directly to **app.writesonic.com/settings/billing**. This page shows your current plan, your renewal date, and your payment method.

**3. Open My Subscription.** This section lists your plan name, the amount, and the next billing date. Check the renewal date before you cancel — if it renews tomorrow, cancelling today and cancelling next week are the same thing, but if it renews in three weeks, you have three weeks of paid access left.

**4. Click Cancel Subscription and confirm.** Writesonic will show a confirmation screen, and may offer a discount or a pause to keep you. If you want to leave, confirm the cancellation. You should see a confirmation that the plan will not renew.

**5. Check your email.** Writesonic sends a confirmation email. If you do not receive one within a few minutes, check your spam folder, and then check the Billing page again — the plan should show as cancelled or as ending on the renewal date.

## Before you cancel: a 5-point checklist

| Check | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Export your drafts and documents | Writesonic may delete your content 30 days after termination under its Terms (section 14.3) — export anything you need first |
| Note your renewal date | You keep access until then, so cancel on your own schedule, not in a panic |
| Check if you are inside the 7-day refund window | If the charge was less than 7 days ago, you can claim a refund — after that, you keep access but do not get money back |
| Check whether you subscribed through Apple or Google Play | App-store subscriptions must be cancelled in the store, not in Writesonic |
| Decide whether you want to pause instead | Some plans offer a pause — useful if you are taking a break but will be back |

## What happens after you cancel

**You keep access until the end of the billing cycle.** Cancelling stops the next charge; it does not cut off your current plan immediately. If you paid monthly and cancel on day 10, you keep the plan until day 30. If you paid annually, you keep it until the anniversary.

**Your content may be deleted after 30 days.** Writesonic's Terms (section 14.3) say the company may delete your account and its contents 30 days after termination. Export your drafts, brand voice settings, and any documents you want to keep before you cancel — or at least within that 30-day window.

**No refund after 7 days.** Writesonic's Terms (section 12.6) give a 7-day refund window from the date of the charge. After that, cancelling stops future charges but does not refund the current period. UK customers have a separate 14-day cooling-off right under UK consumer law — if that applies to you, it is worth knowing, because it is longer than the 7-day window in the Terms.

**Annual plans are different.** If you paid annually and cancel mid-year, you keep access until the anniversary and do not get a pro-rated refund automatically. Writesonic's own guidance says to contact support about annual cancellations — a support ticket is the way to ask about a pro-rated refund, and the answer depends on your case.

## If you subscribed through Apple or Google Play

Writesonic cannot cancel a subscription that Apple or Google is billing. You have to cancel in the store:

- **Apple (iPhone / iPad):** Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Writesonic → Cancel Subscription. Or cancel at reportaproblem.apple.com.
- **Google Play (Android):** Google Play app → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Writesonic → Cancel subscription.

After cancelling in the store, check the Writesonic Billing page to confirm the plan shows as ending. If it still shows as active, contact Writesonic support with your order number.

## Cancel vs delete account — they are different

Cancelling your subscription stops the billing. Deleting your account removes your data. They are separate actions, and deleting your account does not automatically refund you. If you want to leave Writesonic entirely, cancel the subscription first, export your content, and then delete the account from the account settings. Do not delete the account first — you may lose access to the billing page and the refund request form.

## First-hand finding: the refund information conflicts, and the Terms win

While researching this article, we found three different refund claims in circulation: a 7-day window in Writesonic's Terms (section 12.6), a 14-day cooling-off period in UK consumer guidance, and a "no refunds" claim in some third-party roundups. The Terms are the document that governs your purchase, and they say 7 days. The UK 14-day cooling-off right is a separate legal right that applies to UK customers regardless of what the Terms say. The "no refunds" claim is wrong — it appears to come from articles confusing Writesonic's policy with another tool's. The practical rule: if you were charged less than 7 days ago, ask for a refund now, and cite section 12.6 in your support ticket. If you are a UK customer and the charge was less than 14 days ago, cite the cooling-off right as well.

## The bottom line

Cancel from **app.writesonic.com/settings/billing → My Subscription → Cancel Subscription**, confirm, and check your email. You keep access until the end of the cycle. If the charge was less than 7 days ago, claim a refund under Terms section 12.6. Export your drafts first — Writesonic may delete your content 30 days after termination. And if you subscribed through Apple or Google Play, cancel in the store, not in Writesonic.
