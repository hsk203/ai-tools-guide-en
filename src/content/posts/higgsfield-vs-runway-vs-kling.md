**The short answer:** there is no single winner, because the three platforms do not sell the same thing. Higgsfield wins on model choice — Seedance, Kling, Veo, Wan, and more under one credit balance. Runway wins on a coherent, professional workflow with Gen-4.5, Brand Kits, and voice clones, plus the only rollover in this comparison on its Max plan. Kling wins on the cheapest entry price and the cheapest high-volume credits, if you are happy rendering in Kling's own models. The cheapest 10-second clip depends on the model, not the plan: a Kling 3.0 clip on Higgsfield costs about 14 to 15 credits, the same length in Runway's Gen-4.5 costs about 120 credits, and in Kling 4.0 at 1080p it costs about 150 credits at a third-party-reported rate. Pick the model first, then pick the platform that sells it cheapest.

## What each platform actually is

Higgsfield is an aggregator. It puts many video models in one place — Seedance 2.0 and 2.5, Kling 3.0 and 4.0, Veo 3.1, Wan, Grok Video, Nano Banana — under a single credit balance, with commercial use included from the $19-a-month Starter plan upward and no watermark from Starter upward. Its value is choice. Its weakness is that choice makes pricing opaque: a credit buys a different amount of video depending on the model, and the cheapest and most expensive models differ by a factor of five or more for the same 10 seconds.

Runway is a professional video platform built around its own Gen-4 family. It is not an aggregator. Its value is a coherent workflow: generations, projects, storage, Brand Kits, custom voice clones, 4K upscaling, HDR and ProRes on the Max plan, and an MCP integration on Pro. Its pricing is the most transparent of the three, because the flagship rate — Gen-4.5 at 12 credits a second — is stated explicitly on the official pricing page and FAQ. Its weakness is credit burn: at 12 credits a second, the flagship model eats a monthly allowance fast, and only the Max plan lets credits roll over.

Kling is a single-model platform — Kling 4.0, launched 15 October 2026 — sold as a subscription with its own credit system. Its value is price: Standard renews at $8.80 a month, the cheapest paid entry here, and Pro gives 3,000 credits a month for $24.42 a month on annual billing. Its weakness is that its per-second cost is not published by Kling itself; the 10-credits-a-second at 720p and 15 at 1080p figures come from third-party in-app checks, so budgeting requires a test generation. Kling 4.0 generates clips of 3 to 30 seconds at 720p or 1080p, with 4K announced and HDR coming soon, up to 10 keyframes, and native stereo audio.

Read the Kling row carefully. The 10-credits-a-second at 720p and 15 at 1080p figures were checked inside Kling's video generator by Quantslant on 15 October 2026 and corroborated by useapi.net on 5 November 2026. Kling's membership page and 4.0 user guide do not print a per-clip rate, and multiple November 2026 reviews confirm that. So the Kling figures are a third-party in-app check, not an official published rate. Do not budget a large Kling spend without running a test generation first. The same caution applies to Kling 4.0 Flash, a cheaper 720p-only variant reported at 6 credits a second by useapi.net — third-party only. Reseller and API rates use different credit systems and must not be mixed in: Kling's own developer API bills 1 unit a second at 720p and 1.5 at 1080p at about $0.154 a unit, and some resellers quote 7 credits a second at 720p. Those are reseller credits, not Kling subscription credits.

Runway's Turbo rate needs the same honesty. The official page equates 625 credits to 104 seconds and 2,250 credits to 375 seconds — both about 6 credits a second — but equates 9,500 credits to 1,900 seconds, which is about 5 credits a second. The Max line is inconsistent with the other two. Treat Turbo as about 5 to 6 credits a second, with the exact rate unverified, and budget a 10-second Turbo clip at about 50 to 60 credits. Third-party sources variously state 5 credits a second. Do not publish a single exact Turbo rate.

## How many ads does each plan actually buy?

Translate the per-clip costs into a monthly ad count, using 10-second clips as the standard.

**Runway Standard — 625 credits a month, $12 a month on annual billing — and Pro — 2,250 credits, $28 a month.** At Gen-4.5's 12 credits a second, Standard buys about 52 seconds of flagship video a month — about five 10-second clips, one a week with no slack for revisions — while Pro buys about 187 seconds, about 18 clips, or about four a week. At Turbo's roughly 5 to 6 credits a second, Standard buys about 10 to 12 clips and Pro about 37 to 45. Standard is a testing plan for flagship video, not a production plan. Pro is the first Runway plan that fits a real ad cadence, and it adds 1 Brand Kit, 1 custom voice clone, and the Runway MCP integration.

**Runway Max — 9,500 credits a month, $76 a month on annual billing.** At Gen-4.5, Max buys about 791 seconds — about 79 ten-second clips. At Turbo, about 1,580 to 1,900 seconds. Max is the plan for a team producing daily ads in the flagship model, and it is the only plan here with a rollover: up to one month of unused credits carries forward, so a quiet month does not waste the allowance. That rollover is worth real money for seasonal advertisers.

**Kling Pro — 3,000 credits a month, $24.42 a month on annual billing.** At the third-party-reported 15 credits a second at 1080p, Pro buys about 200 seconds — about 20 ten-second 1080p clips. At 10 credits a second at 720p, about 30 clips. Kling Standard at 660 credits buys only about 4 ten-second 1080p clips, or about 6 at 720p. Kling Pro is the value plan here on a per-credit basis, if you are happy with Kling 4.0 as your model and you verify the per-second rate with a test generation first.

**Higgsfield Plus — 1,200 credits a month, $47 a month on annual billing.** At Seedance 2.5's 70 credits per 10 seconds, Plus buys about 17 clips. At Veo 3.1's roughly 73 to 100 credits per 10 seconds, about 12 to 16 clips. At Kling 3.0 720p's 14 to 15 credits per 10 seconds, about 80 clips. Higgsfield's value is not the credit count — 1,200 credits is fewer than Runway Pro's 2,250 and Kling Pro's 3,000 — it is the ability to choose the cheapest model for each clip.

## Head-to-head: product ads versus social ads

Product ads and social ads are not the same job, and the winner changes between them.

**Product ads** — a hero clip for a product page, a demo for an ecommerce listing, a polished spot for a paid campaign — reward quality, control, and consistency. Runway Pro and Max are strong here: Gen-4.5 is expensive at 12 credits a second, but the Brand Kit, voice clone, 4K upscaling, and on Max, HDR and ProRes, give you a professional finish. Higgsfield Plus is strong for a different reason: you can pick Veo 3.1 or Seedance 2.5 for the hero clip and Kling 3.0 for the cut-downs, all under one subscription. Kling Pro is the budget option: Kling 4.0 at 1080p with up to 10 keyframes and native stereo audio can make a good product clip, but you are betting on one model, and the per-second rate is not officially published.

**Social ads** — short, frequent clips for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts — reward volume and speed. Here the per-clip cost dominates. Kling 3.0 via Higgsfield at about 14 to 15 credits per 10 seconds, and Kling 4.0 at a reported 10 credits a second at 720p, are far cheaper per clip than Runway's Gen-4.5 at 120 credits. A creator making 20 social clips a month can do it on Higgsfield Starter's 270 credits in Kling 3.0, or on Kling Standard's 660 credits at 720p, but cannot do it on Runway Standard's 625 credits in Gen-4.5, which buys about five clips. If your social ads need Gen-4.5 quality, budget Runway Pro or Max. If they need volume, budget Higgsfield or Kling and spend the savings on more variants.

Camera control is the feature buyers ask about most after price. Higgsfield markets itself on camera control across models, including Motion Control on Kling 3.0 at 1080p and 4K on the Plus plan. Runway's control story is about the Gen-4 workflow, projects, and brand consistency. Kling 4.0 offers up to 10 keyframes per generation. Test the same prompt on all three before committing: a 10-second test clip costs about 15 credits on Higgsfield in Kling 3.0, about 120 credits on Runway in Gen-4.5, and about 100 to 150 credits on Kling in 4.0 — cheap enough to test, expensive enough to regret skipping the test.

## Limitations / Who Should NOT Buy

**Do not buy Higgsfield if:** you need flagship models on Starter, which excludes Seedance 2.5, Veo 3.1, and Kling 3.0 above 720p; you expect unused credits to roll over, because they do not; or you need a free tier that makes video, because Free has 0 credits and image models only. Do not budget around Katana per-clip costs: an earlier baseline claim of about 154 credits for a roughly 12.7-second clip could not be re-verified from any source found on 2026-11-13, so it is unverified and not published as fact here.

**Do not buy Runway if:** you need high-volume flagship video on a cheap plan — Standard's 625 credits buy only about 52 seconds of Gen-4.5 a month; you need credits to roll over on Standard or Pro, because only Max rolls over, for one month; you need API access, which is reported to be Enterprise-only by third parties; or you are still budgeting around the retired Unlimited plan. Unlimited is gone, replaced by Max. Also, clips are capped around 16 seconds according to third-party sources, so long-form video is not Runway's job.

**Do not buy Kling if:** you need many models in one place — Kling sells Kling 4.0, and if you need Veo or Seedance you need another subscription; you need a published per-second rate to budget with confidence, because Kling does not publish one and the 10 and 15 credits-a-second figures are third-party in-app checks; you are counting on the first-month price, because renewal is materially higher; or you need a free tier that makes 4.0 video, because Basic has no free 4.0 credits, is watermarked, and has no commercial use. Do not buy Kling Pro for 3,000 credits without checking the per-second rate on a test generation first — 3,000 credits is about 20 ten-second 1080p clips at the reported rate, not an unlimited allowance.

## The bottom line

For product and social ads, the decision is model-first, platform-second. If your ads need Runway's Gen-4.5 and its Brand Kit and voice-clone workflow, buy Runway Pro at $28 a month on annual billing for about 18 ten-second flagship clips, or Max at $76 for about 79 clips plus a one-month rollover. If your ads need volume in Kling's models at the lowest price, buy Kling Pro at $24.42 a month on annual billing for 3,000 credits — about 20 ten-second 1080p clips at the third-party-reported rate — and verify that rate with a test first. If your ads need many models under one subscription and you want to pick the cheapest model per clip, buy Higgsfield Plus at $47 a month on annual billing for 1,200 credits — about 17 ten-second Seedance 2.5 clips, or about 80 in Kling 3.0 at 720p — see our [Higgsfield pricing deep dive](/posts/higgsfield-pricing-credit-burn/) for the full per-model math.
