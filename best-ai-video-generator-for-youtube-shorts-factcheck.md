## YouTube Shorts rules (verified 2026-11-18)
- Shorts up to 3 minutes since Oct 15, 2024 (YouTube; confirmed by aishortsguide.com
  and shortfast.com, which cite YouTube Help).
- YPP ad-revenue tier: 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months OR
  10 million public Shorts views in 90 days. Early tier: 500 subs + 3M Shorts views
  or 3,000 watch hours in 90 days (fan funding only). Creators keep 45% of their
  allocated Shorts ad-revenue share.
- July 15, 2025: YouTube renamed "repetitious content" to "inauthentic content";
  mass-produced/template AI content ineligible for monetization; AI use itself not
  banned. Sources: YouTube Help Center via oakgen.ai, shortfast.com, aishortsguide.com
  (all read YouTube's pages Oct 2026).
- AI disclosure: set during upload in YouTube Studio; YouTube says disclosure alone
  does not limit reach or eligibility.
- From Feb 1, 2027: new YPP applicants need 20M Shorts views in 90 days or 8,000
  watch hours; existing partners need 10M Shorts views per 90 days to keep sharing
  Shorts revenue. Sources: YouTube Help via oakgen.ai, lubanai.app, shortfast.com.
